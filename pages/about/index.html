Career

Started as young gifted programmer from the age of 11. I got into creation and engineering even before the age. At 7 I made numerous "inventions" to solve my real world problem. Starting from scratch I build phone mount for action shots using bike helmet, legos, and tapes. Fixed my first personal iPhone at the age of 11 with self sourced replacement parts. No person guiding me except online. Within that same year I fixed our family's broken down pc and made it useable again. Gaining permission to have it to myself I learned game development at 12 years old with first ever program being a simple physics game with functional cube jumping and moving on a platform.

I have never been a type of person to sit still and follow instructions. I build my own instruction manuals. Along with failing is learning. Afterall the failure is just another word for a teacher.

Created simple minigames as part of a larger game in a small start up business taught me what it takes to build real things from scratch. Breaking problems down into systems. Building from small steps leading to something larger. This has lead me to develop a simple yet powerfull way of seeing projects at the young age of 16. Understanding the process of creation has lead me down a rabbit whole of several different projects creation. Multiple in planning phase and couple one actives. For more information I suggest visiting my project page.

Philosophy

I have always believed that curiosity and courage will get you further than pure talent. I value process over result. Learned that since I was very young in life. Result or rewards are just a byproduct of your daily habits and making. Living well and doing things that matter to you will get you further than to those that forces themself to fit the mold. You can be as wobbly as you want. As long as you are yourself and have fun. That is way more important at the end of the day. These beliefs essentially let me to overcome most hardships. Burnout, perfectionism, feeling of obligation, failure and above all. Fear.

As long as you do things that matter to you. You don't have to explain yourself to someone who will not get you. You can also stop at anytime. There is no obligation to do what you don't want to. You can let go. The world will keep on spinning. The flowers will still bloom. The sky will not fall. And things tend to find their way to figure themself out. I am not saying to quit your job immedietly, but you can work towards getting a new one. Something you are more fullfilled with. And again. Failure is just an event. Not a label you hold. You as a person doesn't become less even if you fall.

There is also something about owning things. I hate renting or accessing something that is not mine. This is the reason I use primitive softwares like NeoVim, MP3 player, notebooks and pens. Something about having things in your own term is really important to me. I am not renting stuff. I am not following companies image. I can destroy my notebook anytime. I can write anything to my notebooks without having to fear that the pricing model changes and my entries are locked behind a paywall. I can download music to my mp3 player. Not needing to think about what comes next due to algorithm because I only have what I intend to listen to. No DRM checks. No subscription. Might be just my own want for simpler times when I actually owned things. But I'd rather own things, than paying for access for medias. Libraries are for everyone. So is the internet.
