Pongsakon Naksutti

The Man Himself

Short Summery

" People over product. Honesty over disguised truth. Function over form. That is simply who I am. Moi! Hello in finnish. My name is Pongsakon Tom Naksutti. Your not so average hyper-focused, mildly autism effected, project crazy kid. I built things that matters to me. Not just forms. I will take ugly car over perfect unrideable one any day. I like things from different domains yet all is connected to one single point. Singularity that connects even the most engineering mind to a creative craft. A mixologist that specializes in data management and networking somehow. I don't aim to be perfect. Not even slightly. I don't fit the mold and that is fine. I make the mold that fits me. It has more than enough rooms for me to grow into more wobbly tree than ever, yet too tight to have anyone else's opinion being fitted. And the motto that I will never leave my side is: It is healthier to be crazy than to be perfect "
